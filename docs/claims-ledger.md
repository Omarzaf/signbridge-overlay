# Claims Ledger

Product and contest claims must be backed by named evidence.

| Claim area | Minimum evidence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Language accuracy | Qualified human review of exact released pack | Not established |
| Synchronization | Automated drift and interaction tests | Not established |
| Offline operation | Browser test with network disabled | Not established |
| Small-phone access | Real or representative constrained-device test | Not established |
| Accessibility | Keyboard, screen, touch, captions, and human audit | Not established |
| AI-native workflow | Reproducible Gemini proposal and review evidence | Not established |
| Users or revenue | Consent-safe, arms-length evidence ledger | Not established |

Marketing text, demo narration, and submission material may not outrun this
ledger.
