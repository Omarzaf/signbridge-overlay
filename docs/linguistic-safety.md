# Linguistic Safety

## Authority

A qualified Deaf reviewer for the declared signed language and dialect has final
authority over semantic mappings, nonmanual requirements, corrections, and
publication. AI output, captions, glosses, and hearing-agent judgment do not
replace that authority.

## Required pack metadata

Every SignPack must declare:

- Signed language, region, and dialect.
- Intended audience and educational context.
- Signer and reviewer references using consent-safe identifiers.
- Review status and decision history.
- Unsupported concepts and caption fallbacks.
- Asset license, provenance, hash, and withdrawal state.

## Prohibited behavior

- Publishing AI-generated or agent-inferred mappings without human approval.
- Treating word-for-word gloss substitution as complete translation.
- Cropping or mirroring hands, face, torso, or required nonmanual grammar.
- Hiding unsupported content.
- Claiming completeness or universality beyond collected evidence.

## Release gate

No pack may be represented as linguistically reviewed until the responsible
human reviewer approves the exact hashed assets and manifest being released.
