# Provisional Language Scope

## Selected development scope

- Signed language: American Sign Language
- ISO 639-3 code: `ase`
- Country/region: United States (`US`)
- Audience: educational-video viewers
- Status: provisional; not linguistically approved

“U.S. ASL” is a product-development boundary, not a claim that ASL is uniform.
Regional, racial, cultural, educational, age, and community variation must be
documented with the qualified Deaf reviewer before any mapped sign is released.

## Human gate

Gate 1 remains open until a qualified Deaf ASL reviewer is identified and the
following are recorded privately:

- Basis of qualification and relevant community experience.
- Scope of semantic and nonmanual review authority.
- Compensation and conflicts.
- Consent-safe public identifier or anonymity preference.
- Correction, withdrawal, and escalation process.

No agent may populate the sign catalog, approve a mapping, or label a fixture as
ASL-reviewed before this gate closes.
